## Missing essential facts

- Approved models, SKU/spec sheets, stock and accurate equipment photos.
- Electrical, mounting, dimensions, noise, scheduling, HVAC compatibility and installation instructions.
- Final prices, tax treatment, delivery, fulfillment and quotation validity.
- Oil availability, formulas, approved equipment pairings, pack sizes, prices, safety documentation and sample arrangements.
- Consumption evidence before stating monthly oil budgets.
- Confirmed contact email, phone/WhatsApp, service locations and HUME website URL.
- Support process, warranty and return terms; whether installation is offered at all.
- Form receiver, delivery ownership, privacy contact and retention policies.
- Final site origin and future treatment of the original `/spaces` page.

## Decisions for review

Brand relation is HUME Spaces. Launch structure prioritizes application → equipment → brief → consultation. Oils are scent concepts, not confirmed purchasable SKUs. No subscriptions, maintenance plans, installation promise, testimonials, customer logos, case studies, certification or measurable marketing claims are included. The reference's trial, managed-service, odour neutralisation, hypoallergenic and performance copy was not retained.

Retail and spa pages were added because the reference offers distinct zoning guidance; other application SEO pages are mapped for editorial review rather than reproduced as thin pages. All migration rows are proposals, not live redirects. No claim of outdoor suitability is retained.
