# HUME Spaces: platform and search readiness

Implemented: 11 property solution pages, product comparison, server-rendered platform search, FAQ hub, Organization/WebSite/Product/Article/Breadcrumb/FAQ structured data, sitemap coverage and an optional llms.txt discovery summary. No invented testimonials, prices, certifications or installation promises. The six scents remain concepts.

## Search topics and buyer intent
- Hotel and resort scenting: reception zoning, standalone versus visible tower, indoor versus outdoor areas.
- Gym scenting: extraction, cleaning, member preferences and operating hours.
- Office scenting: reception and lounge, shared air and staff preferences.
- Retail, showrooms and malls: entrance airflow, display volumes and tenant zoning.
- Spas and salons: treatment-product conflicts, reception versus treatment space.
- Cafés: fragrance separation from food and dining; a no-scent option where separation is impossible.
- Clinics: facility policy and sensitivities before product selection; no medical benefit claims.
- Purchase comparison: capacity, controls, listed coverage, approved oils and total quoted costs.

## Launch dependencies
Set SITE_ORIGIN to the confirmed HTTPS domain, deploy with the chosen host, then verify canonical URLs and sitemap. Local previews remain noindex and disallow crawling. Do not create city/service pages until actual coverage is confirmed. Verify specs, photography, oils and service policies before publishing claims.

Connect the domain to Google Search Console, submit sitemap.xml, inspect representative product and solution URLs and confirm generative-AI inclusion preferences. No ranking or AI citation guarantee is made. Schema and llms.txt do not themselves cause recommendations.

## Measurement baseline
The project is local, so there is no public indexing, ranking or AI-citation baseline to measure. After launch, record impressions/clicks by solution page, indexed URL counts, enquiries and opted-in attribution. Inspect server bot/referral logs and establish a repeatable prompt set: hotel lobby scenting, gym scent machine, retail fragrance system, standalone versus HVAC, oil costs and oil compatibility. Record named/cited/absent results monthly, with dated evidence. Analytics providers have not been connected without account details.

## Authority work requiring business input
Add supplier-approved specifications, oil safety/compatibility documents, real project photos and customer-approved case studies. Confirm service locations and warranty/delivery terms. Obtain real editorial or partner mentions; do not fabricate reviews or backlinks.

Official guidance used: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content .
