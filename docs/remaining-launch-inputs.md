Completed in code: production-domain fallback, preview/development crawl protection, canonical/sitemap/robots output, optional consent-based GA4 hooks, saved fragrance cart, discovery attribution, ordering/support clarification.

Owner inputs: supplier-approved model specifications and photos, inventory, oil sizes/formulas/compatibility, the additional fragrance names and notes, quotation prices/taxes, service locations, delivery/installation, warranty/returns, privacy retention terms and real customer-approved case studies.

Analytics: supply NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID (G-...) and rebuild. In GA4 disable all Enhanced Measurement, including outbound links, form interactions and automatic page-view/history measurement. The site sends explicit page paths and events without enquiry fields or query strings. Check consent and DebugView before relying on reports. Events: page_view, whatsapp_enquiry_click, whatsapp_enquiry_prepared, fragrance_selection_change. Clicks are not confirmed leads or sales. No analytics starts without an ID and consent.

Lead operations: record actually received WhatsApp messages in your CRM or a private leads.csv with received date, owner, interest, quote status, next follow-up and outcome. Do not commit personal lead data. Set response ownership and retention terms. A CRM connection requires provider/account details.

After deployment: verify robots allows crawling, sitemap contains humespaces.in HTTPS URLs and canonical/robots metadata are correct. Reinspect representative pages and sitemap in Search Console. The owner holds Search Console and hosting access.

No fabricated reviews, case studies, location coverage, warranties or performance claims have been added. Checkout/accounts/AI chat require a separate confirmed product scope and business systems.
